## Design Context

### Users
Specta is used by brand, growth, and marketing teams who need to understand how their brand appears inside AI answers. Their work context is analytical and evidence-heavy: they compare competitors, inspect citations, review reports, and decide what to optimize next.

### Brand Personality
Professional, evidence-led, calm. The interface should feel like a brand intelligence workspace rather than a generic AI chat product.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a light-first, report-oriented product aesthetic with tinted paper neutrals, restrained teal accents, measured status colors, and fewer decorative effects. Avoid purple-blue AI palettes, glowing cards, glassmorphism, gradient avatars, sparkle/brain/robot symbolism as primary brand signals, and over-rounded component surfaces.

### Visual Identity
The platform signature color is Specta Evidence Teal: `#1F7A6B`.

Use it consistently for:
- Brand recognition moments
- Primary actions
- Active navigation / selected states
- Evidence confirmation or high-confidence analytical emphasis

Do not use it as decoration everywhere. Most of the interface should remain tinted paper neutral so the teal becomes recognizable by restraint.

### Design Principles
- Evidence before magic: emphasize sources, reports, decisions, and traceability instead of AI spectacle.
- Calm working surface: neutral backgrounds carry most of the UI; color is reserved for action, state, and evidence categories.
- Report-grade canvas: Canvas should read like a concise research brief, not a pile of dashboard cards.
- Chat as command surface: messages should feel precise and operational, with muted system state and clear action affordances.
- Token-first styling: prefer shared CSS variables and remove one-off indigo/violet hardcoding from Chat and Canvas.
