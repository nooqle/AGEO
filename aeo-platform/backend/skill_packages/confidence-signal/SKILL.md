# Runtime Guidance

- Treat this package as a source-trust and evidence-quality assessment playbook.
- Focus on what the current citations prove, what they do not prove, and which risks come from weak or thin sources.
- Separate structural quality from factual certainty: source diversity, official coverage, and citation clarity all matter.

# Output Expectations

- Explain confidence as a signal, not an absolute truth claim.
- Surface the strongest and weakest evidence patterns.
- Recommend where the team should improve source quality or citation coverage next.
